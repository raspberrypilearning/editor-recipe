Add more styles and images to upgrade your project.

## Step 1

To change the background colour of your page, add a `background` attribute to your `body` style in `style.css`.

<div class="c-project-code">
--- code ---
---
language: css
line_numbers: true
line_number_start: 1
line_highlights: 2-3
---
body {
  color: tomato;
  background: beige;
}
--- /code ---
</div>
